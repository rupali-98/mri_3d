# Limitations

Research use only, not for diagnosis. Back to the [README](../README.md). The risks these lead to are in [intended use and risk](intended_use_and_risk.md).

- **The training labels do not cover the whole hippocampus.** MSD Task04 was chosen because of GPU limits: its small crops train on a small GPU. But its labels include only "the hippocampus proper and parts of the subiculum" and leave out the thin inner (medial) extensions: the tip of the subiculum, the uncus and a strip along the tail. The model learned exactly that, so it draws a narrower hippocampus than labels that include this tissue. Across all labels, MSD hippocampi are 3–5 mm narrower and 2–3 mm lower than the external ones at the same length (39 vs 38 mm). Training with varying positions does not change this: the missed chunks stay at 0.31 ml per external hippocampus.
- **No external dataset with the same labels.** No public dataset labelled with MSD's anterior/posterior protocol was found. MNI-HISUB25 labels three subfields with a wider outline, so:
  - only the whole hippocampus can be compared, and **the anterior/posterior split cannot be checked against manual labels outside MSD**;
  - the external Dice (0.811) mixes model error with the protocol difference. The model's inner edge stops about 3 mm short of the external label and its front edge reaches about 4 mm further; in 4,663 inner columns the label has tissue where the prediction has none (386 on the outer side).
- **Islands on external scans.** The external box shows more tissue around the hippocampus than MSD crops (about 12 mm below it vs 5 mm), and the model puts small stray pieces there. Keeping the largest piece removes them.
- **Predictions must not be shifted to fit the external labels.** The prediction's centre is offset the same way in every subject, but that is a difference in shape, not position: shifting lowered Dice from 0.840 to 0.794 on held-out subjects (47 of 50 worse).
- **Possible subject overlap between train and test.** MSD gives no subject IDs, so the split and the folds are by file. Neighbouring files (`2k-1`, `2k`) may be the left and right hippocampus of one person; if so, 22 of the 39 test cases have their possible partner in training, and test scores may be optimistic. The external dataset (different people) is an independent check.
- **No subject or side information in MSD.** No subject ID, left/right side, age, sex, diagnosis or scanner per file, and the image positions are reset. So results cannot be compared by side or by group. The 390 files fit one left and one right hippocampus for each of 195 people, but which files belong together is not recorded.
- **The model depends on its surroundings.** Adding an artificial zero border to MSD crops lowered Dice from 0.874 to 0.833, so crops must look like the training crops. On external scans, moving the box ±10 mm in z changes Dice by up to 0.052 (largest piece kept); training with varying positions halves that but does not remove it.
- **Small evidence base.** Each experiment has one single run and one 5-fold cross-validation (fold-to-fold spread up to ±0.016 external Dice), still one seed per fold; the external set is 25 healthy adults from one site and scanner, in MNI space (so its volumes are scaled to a template); the ASHS check covers 4 hippocampi, too few for a p-value; the external label part names were inferred from the atlas.
- **Decisions informed by the external data.** Largest-piece post-processing and the MSD-style box were chosen after looking at external errors, so the external data is no longer a fully independent test. A second, untouched external dataset is needed for that.
